Research Instrument

Student Essay Authenticity Calibration

We are developing a system to help educators identify AI-generated student work. This calibration exercise asks you to read a series of short essays and record your judgment of each one's origin.

Existing automated detection tools have shown inconsistent results across repeated analyses of the same text. This exercise is designed to measure unaided human judgment as a baseline. Please rely solely on your own reading of each passage.

The exercise takes approximately 20 minutes and consists of nine essays on a range of academic subjects, drawn from a mix of human-written and machine-generated sources. No personal information is collected. Your responses are recorded anonymously and may be used in published research on automated detection methods.

Please complete the exercise in a single sitting. Once you submit a rating for an essay, it cannot be revised.
